Subject: Grievance regarding claim deductions for Claim DSH/CL/2026/104552

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally lodge a grievance regarding the partial settlement of my claim under Policy DSH-HF-2026-778812. I was hospitalized at Demo City Hospital, New Delhi, from 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-10. Out of the claimed amount of ₹1,01,500, only ₹58,000 was approved.

Upon reviewing the settlement, I found unjustified deductions and request the payment of the recoverable amount of ₹19,250. The incorrect deductions are as follows:

1. Proportionate cut on Pharmacy - medicines and IV fluids: ₹10,500.
2. Proportionate cut on Laboratory investigations: ₹6,750.
Both of these deductions are invalid as pharmacy and diagnostics can never be part of proportionate deductions. This violates IRDAI circular IRDAI/HLT/REG/CIR/151/06/2020 dated 11 June 2020 (modified product filing guidelines, proportionate deductions), clauses 4-5. I request these amounts to be paid.

3. Settlement doesn't add up: ₹2,000.
The calculation is incorrect. ₹1,01,500 claimed minus ₹41,500 deductions minus ₹0 co-pay equals ₹60,000, but only ₹58,000 was approved. I request this shortfall to be paid.

Please review this claim and release the wrongfully deducted total of ₹19,250. I request a reply and resolution within 14 days. If the matter is not resolved within this timeframe, I may escalate my grievance to Bima Bharosa and the Insurance Ombudsman.
